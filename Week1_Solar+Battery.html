Sample data · simplified demonstration model. One person sleeps 11 pm–7 am and works remotely 9 am–5 pm. The illustrative, unmeasured demand includes continuous background loads, modest morning activity, home-office use, and higher evening activity. Household demand stays positive overnight; grid demand can be zero when solar or storage covers it. Each point represents the average power for one hour (kW × 1 hour = kWh). Solar serves the home first; surplus charges an initially empty battery. Stored solar then covers later shortfalls, with the grid supplying the rest. Battery efficiency is 100%, with no charge or discharge power limits. Surplus beyond battery capacity is exported and excluded from the household supply percentage. Energy left in the battery at midnight is not counted as supplied.
