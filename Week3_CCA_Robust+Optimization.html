Sources & methodology

What the comparisons mean

Sales per account = actual annual retail sales ÷ reported customer accounts. It reflects customer composition and scale; it is not an efficiency measure. Different commercial and industrial mixes can change it.

Calendar-2025 EIA-861 actuals and CEC forecasts are distinct series. Peninsula's actual sales, account count and ratio remain unavailable; its CEC forecast is shown only as a forecast.

Whole-CCA disclosures can include retail sales, other uses and losses. Retail-product disclosures cover the named product only. No shares are multiplied by agency sales. Stacks use a shared 0–101% scale and retain reported values, including totals of 99% or 101%.

Technology rows and official renewable subtotals can differ. Ava totals 101%; San Diego and CleanPowerSF have renewable rounding differences; OCPA reports 54% eligible renewables while technology components sum to 56%. Official figures remain unchanged.

What the workbook does not establish

Technology CAPEX/O&M, illustrative WACC and national capacity factors are benchmarks or assumptions, not CCA operating costs or financing. Regional solar/wind CFs are fleet proxies, not contracted-plant performance; they appear only as labeled context in details.

No plant identities, locations, capacities or generation are inferred. Service-area descriptions refer to reported 2025 coverage; exact historic boundaries are not confirmed.

Partial disclosures remain partial. Sonoma's 50% aggregate renewable summary is shown without inventing its technology split; its 1% general-system/unspecified entry is counted once. Redwood's renewable-plus-carbon-free claim is not converted into a renewable percentage.

Source links are workbook references and may need internet access. Source claims have not been independently verified.

Sources & methodology

Scope of protection

Only annual FixedCost coefficients are uncertain, with deviations of +20% of nominal. The budget limits simultaneous adverse exposure. This does not protect against uncertain weather, demand or fuel costs, and does not guarantee improved reliability.

Technologies: Geo, Coal, CCGT, CT, Wind and Solar. Original hourly demand and renewable availability; nonnegative capacity, generation and unmet demand; curtailable renewables; $9,000/MWh unmet-demand penalty. No storage or CCA data.

min nominal fixed + variable + unmet costs + Γ θ + Σ excess[i] θ ≥ 0; excess[i] ≥ 0 θ + excess[i] ≥ 0.20 FixedCost[i] CAP[i] Independent worst-case increment: sum largest floor(Γ) cost exposures + fractional Γ × next largest exposure

Exported validation

Optimal and feasible statuses, demand balance, generation limits, nonnegativity, cost reconstruction and independent worst-case increments passed. Γ=0 matches the verified nominal objective; Γ=6 matches deterministic FixedCost ×1.20. Objectives are nondecreasing with Γ; equivalence compares objectives, not identical solutions.

Validation compares objective values rather than requiring identical plans when multiple optimal solutions exist.

Nominal-reference capacity and dispatch are held fixed for same-budget evaluations; fixed-cost uncertainty does not affect dispatch. The deterministic +20% run is a validation reference only.
