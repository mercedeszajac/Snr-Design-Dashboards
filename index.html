Energy Systems Project

A weekly collection of interactive dashboards exploring electricity demand, generation planning, California energy data, and optimization. Select a week below to explore the work.

Week 1

Solar & Battery Explorer

Goal: Explore how household electricity demand, solar generation, and battery storage interact.

An introductory interactive dashboard showing how changes in energy settings affect a household’s electricity balance.

What’s included

  • Household demand and solar generation profiles
  • Battery storage and grid electricity interaction
  • Interactive settings for exploring different conditions

Week 2

Capacity Expansion Overview

Goal: Visualize the generation planning concepts explored in Notebook 3.

A dashboard introducing how technology costs and electricity demand influence generation capacity decisions.

What’s included

  • Notebook 3 capacity expansion concepts and results
  • Generation technology comparisons
  • Investment and operating cost tradeoffs
Open Week 2 dashboard

Week 3

CCA Comparison & Robust Optimization

Goal: Explore California Community Choice Aggregator data and examine planning under cost uncertainty.

Two separate analyses: a comparison of 13 CCAs and robust optimization results using the Notebook 3 course dataset.

What’s included

  • California CCA data and interactive comparisons
  • Generation capacity and technology information where available
  • Robust optimization results for Gamma 0, 2, and 6
  • Costs and investment decisions under fixed-cost uncertainty
Open Week 3 dashboard

Week 4

California Capacity Expansion

Goal: Apply capacity expansion modeling to sourced California system inputs.

Two validated hypothetical CAISO greenfield benchmarks: gas-only and gas plus renewables. The renewable case combines 2025 demand, 2026 candidate availability, and 2022 weather profiles.

What’s included

  • Optimized capacity, annual generation, and domestic modeled costs
  • Hourly dispatch and modeled unmet demand
  • Interactive demand and renewable profile exploration
  • Sources, assumptions, validation, and downloadable summaries
  • Documented data gaps preventing a brownfield solution
Open Week 4 dashboard
Each dashboard represents the scope and assumptions of its weekly study. Modeled results are hypothetical planning outcomes. Sources and limitations are provided within the dashboards where available.
